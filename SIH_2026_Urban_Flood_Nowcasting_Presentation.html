🌊 Detailed Explanation of Solution
  • • Multi-Agent AI Platform: Replaces heavy physical 2D Navier-Stokes/SWMM solvers with a high-speed Physics-Informed ML surrogate regressor & classifier.
  • • Sub-Second Inundation Nowcasts: Predicts node-level manhole surcharge volumes and road waterlogging depths across 50+ junctions in <5 milliseconds.
  • • MapLibre GL 3D Visualization: Live interactive web dashboard rendering hazardous road segments, elevation contours, and Doppler radar sweeps without proprietary API fees.
🎯 How it Addresses the Problem
  • • Solves the 15-Minute Warning Gap: Sudden cloudbursts flood Jankipuram underpasses before traditional tools can even finish running their 45-min models.
  • • Eliminates Vehicle Submergence: Actively identifies impassable roads (>0.30m water depth) and routes commuters through verified safe arterial paths.
  • • Targeted Municipal Pump Deployment: Alerts Lucknow Municipal Corporation (LMC) to deploy mobile suction pumps 20 mins before peak waterlogging.
💡 Innovation & Uniqueness
  • • Physics-Informed Surrogacy: Infuses Manning’s hydraulic equations and the Rational Runoff formula into Gradient Boosted Trees for high fidelity ($R^2 > 0.95$).
  • • Decoupled Multi-Agent Engine: Autonomous Ingestion, Inference, and Alert/Routing agents running asynchronously on lightweight hardware.
  • • Flood-Penalized Dynamic Dijkstra: Reweights street graph edges in real time based on continuous inundation depth ($W = L \times [1 + 50 \cdot D^2]$).
